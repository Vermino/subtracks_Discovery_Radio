## 2024-10-24 - The "Cancel" Shuffle Button & Missing Labels
**Learning:** Found a shuffle button labeled "Cancel" and primary playback controls missing accessibility labels entirely. This shows that copy-paste errors can lead to confusing UX, and even critical controls can be missed during accessibility checks.
**Action:** Always verify tooltips match the icon/action, especially for FABs. Check primary interaction points (like play/pause) for missing semantic labels first.
